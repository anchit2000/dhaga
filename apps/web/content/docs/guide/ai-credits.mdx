Every cloud-AI action in Dhaga costs **credits**. `/app/settings` → **Credits**
is the page that tells you how many you have left, where the rest went, and
what you did to spend them.

![The Credits tab in Dhaga settings: a "Credits this month" card showing 33 of 85 credits left with a used-bar, a reset date, and a Monthly allowance / Credits added for you / Total breakdown, above a "Where your credits went" card listing deep research, card scans, Ask Dhaga, notes, briefs, drafts, quick adds, and free watchlist scans with their counts and credit costs](/docs/guide/credits.png)

## How many are left

The first card is the headline: **credits remaining out of your total for the
month**, a bar showing how much of it you have used, and the date the counter
resets. Allowances do not roll over — a fresh month starts from the full
number.

Every plan has an allowance, including the free one: a **free account gets 10
credits a month**. That is ten card scans, or five scans and five notes, or five
questions to Ask Dhaga — enough to keep capturing with AI, not enough to lean on
it all month.

If your plan has no monthly ceiling, the card says **Unlimited** and shows what
you have used so far instead of a number left.

If something has been *added* to your allowance, the card breaks it down rather
than just showing a bigger number:

- **Monthly allowance** — what your plan (or this instance's configuration)
  includes.
- **Promotion this month** — replaces the line above when whoever runs the
  instance is running a promotional month.
- **Credits added for you** — a one-off grant, e.g. after something went wrong
  on their side. Grants are always *added on top*; they never rewrite what you
  spent.

## Where your credits went

The second card is the breakdown for the current month: one row per kind of
action, how many of them you ran, and what they cost. **The rows add up to the
total** — that is the same number your monthly cap is measured against, so
nothing is counted twice or quietly left out.

Credits are charged per **action**, not per model call — a card scan that takes
two round-trips behind the scenes is still one scan:

| Action | Credits |
|---|---|
| Card scan | 1 |
| Quick add (pasted text or a signature) | 1 |
| Note (text) | 1 |
| Follow-up draft | 1 |
| Ask Dhaga (a question) | 2 |
| Pre-meeting brief | 2 |
| Deep research (enrich from the web) | 20 |
| Goal match, requested now | 3 |
| Watchlist scan (nightly) | Free |
| Contact check (nightly) | Free |
| Goal match (nightly) | Free |

A **photo note** costs 2 — reading the image and extracting from the resulting
text happen as two separate actions.

**Deep research** is the expensive one on purpose: it pays for live web
searches on top of the model, and it is the only action whose real cost is
dominated by something other than Dhaga's own tokens.

**Watchlist scans are free.** They appear in the breakdown marked *Free* rather
than being hidden, so a month of nightly scanning is visible and accounted for
without costing you anything. You never asked for any individual scan — how
many people you can watch is what limits them instead.

**Contact checks and goal matches are free for the same reason.** A *contact
check* is the nightly read of one contact to work out whether it is a person
you could message or a shop your address book dragged in; a *goal match* weighs
one contact against the goal you wrote in your own words. You never asked for
either judgement about any particular person, so neither is billed — what
limits them is a ceiling on how many contacts a single night's sweep may look
at, not your allowance. A whole imported address book is worked through over a
few nights that way, and every one of those actions still shows up in the
breakdown marked *Free* rather than being hidden.

**Asking for a goal match *now* is the exception, and it costs 3 credits.**
Setting or rewording a goal is always free and never runs the model — the
nightly pass picks it up and the people who fit are there the next day. If you
don't want to wait, **Request now** on the goal line runs the match while you
watch, and Dhaga tells you what it costs before it spends anything. It shows up
in the breakdown as *Goal match (requested)*, separately from the free nightly
ones, so you can always see which of the two you paid for.

<Callout title="Credits aren't the only gate">
Deep research costs more than a free month holds, and **deep research and
pre-meeting briefs are Pro-and-up features** anyway — having credits left over
doesn't unlock them. What a free allowance realistically buys is card scans,
notes, quick add, follow-up drafts and questions to Ask Dhaga.
</Callout>

## Recent activity

The last card is a plain list of your AI actions — *"Card scan · 1 credit · 2
hours ago"* — newest first. It starts by showing your most recent actions, and
a **Load more** button at the bottom fetches further back, so you can page all
the way through your entire usage history, not just what you did this week.
It is not limited to the current month either, so opening the page on the 1st
still shows you what you last did.

<Callout title="This page is yours alone">
Everything here is derived from your own usage records. Whoever runs the
instance has a separate [admin
screen](/docs/self-hosting#instance-wide-ai-credit-controls-appadminai-credits-hostedee)
for setting allowances and granting credits — it never shows them what you
asked or what you captured.
</Callout>

## Running out

When the month's credits are gone, you find out **before** you click: the AI
buttons are greyed out, with the reason sitting next to them — *"You're out of
AI credits this month — all 10 used. They reset on the 1st."* — and a **See
credits** link back to this page. Nothing fails silently, and nothing is charged
for an action you didn't get.

Greyed out until the counter resets: **Extract contact** on the paste tab,
**Scan card**, **Ask Dhaga**, **Brief me**, **Draft follow-up**, **Enrich
from public web**, and confirming a goal match with **Request now** (the goal
itself still saves, and tonight's free pass still runs).

Everything that doesn't spend credits keeps working exactly as before — manual
capture and editing, adding a fact or a follow-up by hand, search by name, the
graph, import, export, and voice dictation (which runs on your own device).
Adding photos to a card-scan tray still works too; only the **Scan** button
itself is greyed.

**Notes are deliberately never greyed out**, typed or photo — writing something
down must not depend on having credits. What you had already typed is never
thrown away either: save a note with an empty balance and it still saves — you
just get told what didn't happen: *"Note saved.
You're out of AI credits this month, so facts weren't extracted."* (a photo note
says *"Photo note saved…"*). You can add facts by hand, or re-run extraction on
that note once the counter resets.

Seeing *"No monthly AI credits on this plan — upgrade to enable AI actions"*
instead? That is a cap of **0**, which only happens when whoever runs the
instance has deliberately set your allowance there. It is not what a free
account normally looks like.

Self-hosting? `DHAGA_AI_MONTHLY_CAP` seeds your instance's monthly allowance —
it applies until an admin sets a number in the app, and from then on the stored
number is the one that counts. See [Self-hosting](/docs/self-hosting).
