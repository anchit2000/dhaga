title: Capturing people
description: Add a person to your graph from pasted text, a card photo, voice, your webcam, or a file — with a review step that confirms the fields before saving.
---

Capture is one click from anywhere. An **Add** button in the app nav opens the
capture dialog on **every** `/app` screen — the fastest path in. The same
capture also lives in the **capture dock** at the bottom of every screen and on
the dedicated **`/app/quick-add`** page, which additionally lets you attach the
new person to an existing event (below). All run the same extraction, keep the
original input as a **receipt**, and meter AI usage per account. A card scan or
a paste-to-contact costs **1 credit**, and the free tier includes **10 AI
credits a month** — ten card scans, say, or five scans and five notes. Run out
and the app stays fully usable for manual capture. See
[AI credits](/docs/guide/ai-credits).

## Quick-add modes

The capture panel opens on the **Manual** tab — the fastest, no-AI path, and the
one that keeps working when you're out of credits, or on a self-hosted instance
with no API key. Three
pills across the top switch between **Manual**, **Paste text**, and **Card
photo**.

- **Manual** (the default) — type the person in by hand with no AI. Manual has
  its own sub-tabs to add a **person**, a **relationship** between two people, or
  a **fact / follow-up**. See [Adding someone manually](#adding-someone-manually)
  below.

![Dhaga manual add-person form with fields for name, jobs, emails, phones, and links](/docs/guide/add-person.png)

Switch to an AI capture mode for however else you captured the person:

- **Paste text** — drop in an email signature, the text off a business card, or a
  LinkedIn intro, and Dhaga pulls out the name, title, company, and contact
  methods. On this tab (outside the home dock) a **Voice** mic sits under the
  box: tap it and describe who you met; the dictation streams live into the text
  and is extracted the same way. With Dhaga Voice the transcript comes back as
  tappable word-chips — tap a mis-heard word to fix it, and Dhaga learns that
  spelling for next time.

![Dhaga quick-add in paste-text mode, ready to accept an email signature or card text](/docs/guide/quick-add-paste.png)

<Callout title="Voice needs WebGPU">
Dhaga Voice dictation runs entirely on-device, so it needs **WebGPU** — available
today in desktop Chrome and Edge. On iOS Safari and other browsers without WebGPU,
Dhaga detects this up front and shows a clean "voice isn't supported on this
device — type it in instead" message rather than failing mid-recording. Manual
entry is always available.
</Callout>

- **Card photo** — take or upload a photo of a business card. Dhaga runs OCR and
  also reads a LinkedIn QR code if one is on the card. You're not limited to one
  shot: add several photos of the same card or leaflet — front and back, or
  multiple pages — in a tray where you can reorder, crop, or remove any of them
  before scanning. On desktop you can also shoot straight from a live webcam,
  capturing multiple frames in one sitting. Every image you add merges into a
  **single** contact, and each photo is kept as its own source receipt. Where a
  card marks a number or address — *Mobile*, *Direct*, *Office*, a branch name —
  that marking is carried into the contact's phone and email labels, so you
  don't have to retype which is which.

The capture dock at the bottom of the screen adds two more shortcuts:

- **Camera** — shoot one or several frames straight from your webcam. **Done**
  drops them into the card-photo tray, so you can crop, reorder, or remove any
  of them before pressing Scan.
- **Upload** — attach an image or file you already have.

![Dhaga quick-add in card-photo mode with a "Take or choose card photos" drop area (front, back, or multiple pages all merge into one contact), a Use live camera button, and the Voice / Camera / Upload capture dock](/docs/guide/quick-add-capture.png)

<Callout title="AI extraction needs a key">
The card scan, voice, and text extraction run through Claude. On a self-hosted
instance without `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, capture falls back to simpler heuristic
parsing. See [Self-hosting](/docs/self-hosting) for the env var.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Prefer your phone?">
You can also forward a contact card, note, or photo straight to Dhaga on
WhatsApp or Telegram — no app to install. See
[Messaging capture](/docs/guide/messaging-capture).
</Callout>

## Review, disambiguate, and save

Extraction never saves silently. A **review dialog** shows the fields it pulled
so you can correct them before the person lands in your graph:

1. Check the name, title, company, emails, phones, and links.
2. If the details look like someone you already have, Dhaga flags the match so
   you can merge into the existing contact instead of creating a duplicate.
3. Optionally **attach the person to an event** right from the review dialog —
   handy when you're clearing a stack of cards from one conference. See
   [Groups & events](/docs/guide/groups-and-events).
4. Save.

## Adding someone manually

Manual is the **default** tab — the capture panel opens on it, so typing a person
in by hand (no AI, no API key) is the zero-click path. Its sub-tabs let you add a
**person**, record a **relationship** between two people you already have, or log
a **fact / follow-up** against a contact. Switch to **Paste text** or **Card
photo** whenever you'd rather have Dhaga extract from captured input, or go
straight to **`/app/people/new`** for the full person form.

![The Manual hub with its Person, Relationship, and Fact / follow-up sub-tabs](/docs/guide/quick-add-manual.png)

The person form keeps **Experience** and **Education** as two separate sections:

- **Experience** — roles with a title, company, dates, and a **relationship
  type** (employment, internship, founder, board member, advisor, volunteer).
- **Education** — schooling with an **institution**, **degree / programme**,
  **field of study**, years, and a **currently studying** toggle. Education is
  stored as a "studied at" affiliation, so schools become nodes in your
  [graph](/docs/guide/exploring-the-graph) just like employers.

Add as many of each as you need, along with several labelled emails and phones
and links, then save.

![The manual person form's Experience and Education sections — Education showing Institution, Degree / programme, Field of study, year fields, a "Currently studying here" toggle, and a relationship-type selector](/docs/guide/add-person-education.png)
