title: Capturing people
description: Add a person to your graph from pasted text, a card photo, voice, your webcam, or a file — with a review step that confirms the fields before saving.
---

Capture is one click from anywhere. An **Add** button in the app nav opens the
capture dialog on **every** `/app` screen — the fastest path in. The same
capture also lives in the **capture dock** at the bottom of every screen and on
the dedicated **`/app/quick-add`** page, which additionally lets you attach the
new person to an existing event (below). All run the same extraction, keep the
original input as a **receipt**, and meter AI usage per account. Cloud AI is a
paid feature (Pro and up) — the free tier has no cloud AI, though the app stays
fully usable for manual capture.

## Quick-add modes

Open `/app/quick-add`. It defaults to **paste text** — drop in an email
signature, the text off a business card, or a LinkedIn intro, and Dhaga pulls
out the name, title, company, and contact methods.

![Dhaga quick-add in paste-text mode, ready to accept an email signature or card text](/docs/guide/quick-add-paste.png)

Switch modes for however you captured the person:

- **Card photo** — take or upload a photo of a business card. Dhaga runs OCR and
  also reads a LinkedIn QR code if one is on the card. You're not limited to one
  shot: add several photos of the same card or leaflet — front and back, or
  multiple pages — in a tray where you can reorder, crop, or remove any of them
  before scanning. On desktop you can also shoot straight from a live webcam,
  capturing multiple frames in one sitting. Every image you add merges into a
  **single** contact, and each photo is kept as its own source receipt.
- **Voice** — tap the mic and describe who you met; the dictation is
  transcribed and extracted. With Dhaga Voice, the transcript comes back as
  tappable word-chips — tap a mis-heard word to fix it, and Dhaga learns that
  spelling for next time.
- **Camera** — grab a shot straight from your webcam.
- **Upload** — attach an image or file you already have.

![Dhaga quick-add in card-photo mode with a "Take or choose card photos" drop area (front, back, or multiple pages all merge into one contact), a Use live camera button, and the Voice / Camera / Upload capture dock](/docs/guide/quick-add-capture.png)

<Callout title="AI extraction needs a key">
The card scan, voice, and text extraction run through Claude. On a self-hosted
instance without `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, capture falls back to simpler heuristic
parsing. See [Self-hosting](/docs/self-hosting) for the env var.
</Callout>

## Review, disambiguate, and save

Extraction never saves silently. A **review dialog** shows the fields it pulled
so you can correct them before the person lands in your graph:

1. Check the name, title, company, emails, phones, and links.
2. If the details look like someone you already have, Dhaga flags the match so
   you can merge into the existing contact instead of creating a duplicate.
3. Optionally **attach the person to an event** right from the review dialog —
   handy when you're clearing a stack of cards from one conference. See
   [Groups & events](/docs/guide/groups-and-events).
4. Save.

## Adding someone manually

You don't have to leave the capture screen for this. Under **every** capture
mode — both **Paste text** and **Card photo** — a **"Prefer to type it in? Add
manually"** link swaps the extractor for a blank contact form, so you can enter
someone by hand with no AI. Or go straight to **`/app/people/new`**. Either way
you can add multiple jobs, several labelled emails and phones, and links, then
save.
