**`/app/graph`** renders your entire network on a single canvas. Every person
and connection you've captured is one node in a graph you can pan, zoom, and
interrogate.

![The Dhaga graph canvas showing the full network settled into a layout, with the warm-path panel in the top-left corner](/docs/guide/graph-overview.png)

## Find a warm path

The **Warm path** finder answers "how do I get introduced to this person?"
Enter a target and Dhaga renders the **intro chains** that connect you to them
through people you both know — for example *You → Bhavna Costa → Aarav Bose*.

![The warm-path panel listing three intro chains from you to a target person through mutual connections](/docs/guide/graph-warm-path.png)

## Inspect a node

Click any node to open its **side panel**. It shows that person's edges — split
into **incoming** and **outgoing** — and lets you:

- **Add a relationship** to another person — search your graph, or add a new
  person on the spot if they aren't in it yet.
- **Open** their full profile page.
- **Isolate their circle** to focus on just their immediate connections.

![The graph with a node selected, its side panel showing incoming and outgoing edge counts, and the focused node's circle isolated](/docs/guide/graph-node-panel.png)

## Reading the edges

Edges are **directional**, and hovering a node makes the direction obvious: its
**outgoing** edges — the ones where it's the subject — thicken into solid amber
with a clear arrowhead, while edges merely **pointing at it** stay softer and
thinner. So *Rohan → parent of → Priya* reads at a glance.

Employment and schooling both draw an edge from the person to the organisation:

- A current employer is the person's primary **"works at"** edge.
- Past employers render as their own **"worked at"** edges — every role in a
  person's history shows, not just the current one.
- Education renders as **"studied at"** (or "attended").

![A hovered person node on the graph with its outgoing edges thickened into solid amber arrows and its incoming edges rendered softer and thinner](/docs/guide/graph-edge-direction.png)

## Layers and controls

- The **Layers** panel toggles which node types and circles are drawn, so you
  can declutter a dense graph.
- **Zoom**, **fit**, and **reset** controls frame the view — fit to see
  everything, reset to return to the default layout.

Non-person nodes like gyms or projects show up here too — see
[Custom entities](/docs/guide/entities).

## See who is nearby

Open **`/app/map`** to see contacts whose saved locations can be placed on a
city-grain map. Tap a pin to open the people at that place. The coverage line
below the map separates contacts that are mapped, still being placed, could not
be placed, or have no location, so the visible pins never pretend to be your
whole network. Dhaga uses only locations already in your graph; there is no live
tracking.

![The contact map loaded with a city pin, zoom controls, attribution, and coverage counts](/docs/guide/scheduling/map-light-desktop.webp)
