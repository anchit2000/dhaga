**Dhaga** turns everyone you meet into a private knowledge graph you own. You
capture a person once — from a card photo, a pasted email signature, a voice
note — and Dhaga keeps the source as a receipt, extracts the facts, and wires
them into a graph you can search, ask questions of, and explore visually.

This guide walks through the app the way you actually use it. Each page is a
short "how to" for one part of the product.

## Your daily briefing

`/app` opens on the Home briefing — the short list of **threads to pull
today**, alongside the follow-ups you set and the people you touched most
recently. Dhaga weighs everyone in your graph against everything it knows is
actually happening — a keep-in-touch rhythm that has come due, a follow-up
dated for today, a birthday landing, a job change you asked to watch for, how
long the silence has run, how connected someone is — and shows you the few at
the top. Every row says why it's there:

- **Check-in** — someone you keep in touch with daily.
- **Due** — your keep-in-touch rhythm for this person has run out.
- **Follow-up** — something you promised to do is due today, or is late.
- **Occasion** — a birthday or anniversary, today or just ahead.
- **Signal** — a job change or news story from your watchlist.
- **Quiet** — no reason beyond the silence; you simply haven't spoken in a
  long while.
- **Network** — nothing is due at all; they're well connected in your graph
  and worth a thread anyway.

Being **starred** nudges someone up the list, and the list rotates gently day
to day so the same well-connected face isn't there every morning — but neither
ever becomes the stated reason. "Last spoke" means more than the button:
writing a note about someone, or scanning them at an event, resets their
rhythm exactly as **Reached out** does. Each row has a one-tap **Reached out**
button so the list stays honest, and on a day already full of meetings the
list gets shorter — never empty.

Once your graph isn't empty, a **graph at a glance** strip
renders beneath the briefing — quick counts of People, Companies, Notes, Facts,
Relationships, Events, Open follow-ups, and Entities. When AI extraction needs a
decision from you, a **Confirmations** preview tile surfaces the count of pending
confirmations waiting on your yes/no. The capture dock sits at
the bottom of every screen —
**Voice**, **Camera**, **Upload**, and **Capture** — so you can add someone the
moment you meet them.
