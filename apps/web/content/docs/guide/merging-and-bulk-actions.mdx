Your **People** list isn't only for browsing. Select several people and act on
them in one go, or fold two records of the same person into a single one.

## Select people

On **`/app/people`**, every row has a checkbox, and the header checkbox selects
everyone on the current page. Your selection **sticks as you page through** the
table, so you can gather people across several pages before acting. The moment
one person is selected, a **bulk-action bar** appears.

## Act on the selection

With people selected, the bar offers:

- **Merge** — combine two or more records of the same person into one (needs at
  least two selected). See below.
- **Add to company** — attach everyone to a company at once; pick an existing
  one or create it inline.
- **Tag** — add or remove a tag across the whole selection.
- **Star** / **Unstar** — flag or unflag people for your Saved list.
- **Delete** — forget the selected people. This cascades — their notes, facts,
  edges, and embeddings go too — exactly like "forget this person" on a single
  profile.

## Merge duplicate contacts

Met the same person twice — a card scan and a LinkedIn import, say — and now
they're two records? **Merge** folds them into one:

1. Select the records and click **Merge** (or start from **Find duplicates**,
   below).
2. **Pick the survivor** — the record everything else folds into.
3. **Resolve conflicts.** Where records disagree on a single-value field —
   **name**, **nickname**, or **location** — you choose which value wins. Fields
   where they already agree don't ask.
4. Everything that can hold more than one value is **combined** onto the
   survivor: emails, phones, links, addresses, important dates, custom fields,
   tags, jobs, notes, facts, and relationship edges. Nothing is dropped.

The other records are then **permanently deleted**. It all happens in a single
step — either the whole merge lands or nothing changes.

<Callout type="warn" title="Merging can't be undone">
The records you merge away are hard-deleted, with no undo — the same model as
"forget this person." Their data lives on in the survivor, but the separate
records are gone. Make sure you've picked the right survivor first.
</Callout>

## Find duplicates

You don't have to spot duplicates yourself. The **Find duplicates** link in the
People page header opens **`/app/people/duplicates`**, which groups likely
duplicates it notices — by a **shared email**, a **shared phone**, or a
**similar name** — into clusters. Each cluster has a **Review & merge** button
that opens the merge dialog prefilled with that group, so cleaning up is two
clicks.
