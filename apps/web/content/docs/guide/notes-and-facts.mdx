title: Notes & facts
description: Add notes to a person by typing, by voice, or by photo; Dhaga extracts structured facts automatically (or add facts and follow-ups by hand — no AI needed), keeps a receipt on each, and cascades deletions.
---

Everything you know about a person lives on their profile at
**`/app/people/[id]`** — their jobs, contact methods, location, relationships,
and, below that, their **notes and facts**.

![The top of a person's profile showing their jobs, email, location, and relationships](/docs/guide/person-detail.png)

## Add a note

In the **Notes** box on a person's page, either type what you remember or tap
the mic and just talk — voice notes are transcribed for you. The box hints as
much: *"Type — or tap the mic and just talk. Facts get extracted
automatically."* With Dhaga Voice, the transcript comes back as tappable
word-chips so you can fix any mis-heard word — doing so teaches Dhaga that
spelling for next time. Or [photograph it](#add-a-note-from-a-photo) — the
third way into the same box.

![A person's profile showing the Facts section with two extracted facts, the Enrich from public web button, and the Notes box with Add note and Voice note buttons](/docs/guide/person-facts-enrich.png)

## Add a note from a photo

Some things are quicker to photograph than to write down: a whiteboard after a
session, a conference poster, a page of handwritten notes, a receipt from the
dinner, the badge someone was wearing.

Tap **Photo note** in the same Notes box and pick or shoot the photo. Dhaga
reads the text out of it and **that text becomes the note** — so it is searchable,
it shows in the person's timeline, and it goes through exactly the same
extraction as anything you type: facts, follow-ups and relationships, each with
a receipt pointing back at this note.

- **Several photos, one note.** Add up to six — pages of the same handout, or
  two halves of a whiteboard. They are read in tray order, so reorder them with
  the arrows if you shot them out of sequence, and crop out anything that isn't
  the subject.
- **Add your own line too.** Anything you type in the box is kept above the
  transcription, as your framing of why the photo matters.
- **The photo is kept as the receipt.** It appears under **Photos** on the
  person's page, alongside any scanned cards, unless you have turned
  [photo storage off in Settings](/docs/guide/settings) — in which case only the
  transcription is kept. Deleting the note deletes its photos with it.

Reading a photo is a cloud-AI action, so it costs credits like any other — 2 in
total, since reading the image and extracting facts from it are two actions.
With no credits left, or on an instance with no AI configured, type the note
instead — a photo alone has nothing to save.

## Capture a note from quick-add

You don't have to be on someone's page to note something about them. Type a
free-text note into [quick-add](/docs/guide/capturing-people) — *"Met Priya,
discussed the Q3 pilot"* — and Dhaga recognises it as a note **about a person
you already have**, not a brand-new contact to create.

It matches the name against your contacts and attaches the note to that person's
timeline, where its facts and relationships get extracted just like any note you
add on their page.

- **One clear match** and it attaches straight away — *"Added a note to
  Priya"*, nothing to click.
- **Unsure** — several people could be the *Priya* you meant, or nobody matches
  at all — and Dhaga asks first instead of guessing, showing a
  [who-is-this-about card](/docs/guide/confirmations#who-is-this-note-about) right
  in quick-add so you can pick the right person or add a new one on the spot.

## Facts get extracted automatically

When you save a note *and you have AI credits left this month*, Dhaga extracts
structured **facts** from it — for example a personal detail or an intent
("wants an intro to a designer"). Each fact is tagged by kind (**PERSONAL**,
**INTENT**, and so on).

Every extracted fact carries a **receipt**: the source note it came from, shown
as *"FROM NOTE, 23/7/2026"*. This is a core principle — no AI-derived fact
exists without a traceable source you can open and verify.

Extraction runs **in the background**, and the note is saved the moment you
press **Add note** — so you never have to sit and watch it. A small pill above
the Facts section reports progress (*"Extracting facts…"*), and it keeps running
if you navigate away or close the tab: the facts are written server-side
regardless of who is watching. While you're on the page, the pill turns into a
short confirmation of what landed — *"Extraction finished — 4 facts and 1
follow-up added."* — and clears itself. If a run fails, the pill says so and
offers **Retry**.

If you've already moved on, the finished run reaches you through the
[**bell**](/docs/guide/calendar#staying-reminded) in the top nav instead —
*"Extracted 4 facts and 1 follow-up from your note about Priya"* — and clicking
it takes you back to the person. Runs that failed, or that stopped because
you're out of AI credits, say so there too, so a job never finishes in silence.

A note costs **1 credit** to extract (a photo note costs 2 — reading the image
and extracting from it are two actions). Run out and the note is still saved,
with the shortfall spelled out: *"Note saved. You're out of AI credits this
month, so facts weren't extracted."* Add the facts by hand below, or re-run
extraction after the counter resets. See
[AI credits](/docs/guide/ai-credits).

## Add facts and follow-ups by hand

You don't need cloud AI to build a person's profile. When your credits are gone
— or any time you'd rather just type it — use **＋ Add fact** in the Facts
section and
**＋ Add follow-up** in the Follow-ups section. Manual facts have no source note
(nothing to trace back to) and are saved as already-verified, since you entered
them yourself. Everything else in Dhaga — contacts, jobs, contact methods,
relationships, groups — is fully editable by hand too, so the app is completely
usable with no AI at all.

<Callout title="This is the user's data about third parties">
Facts are extracted from *your* notes about the people you meet. Nothing is
collected silently — a fact only appears because you wrote the note it came
from.
</Callout>

## Editing, deleting, and the cascade

- **Edit or delete a fact** directly if the extraction got something wrong or
  the detail no longer applies.
- **Re-run extraction** on a note (the rotate icon on the note row) if you
  edited it or the first pass missed something — this replaces the note's
  derived facts and follow-ups rather than duplicating them.
- **Delete the source note** and every fact derived from it is tombstoned along
  with it — the receipt guarantees Dhaga knows exactly which facts to remove.
- **Delete the person** and the cascade runs all the way down: notes, facts,
  graph edges, and embeddings all go with them.

The one exception is the receipt note left behind by an import, a card scan, a
QR scan, or a WhatsApp capture: it can't be edited, deleted, or re-run through
extraction. It is not something you wrote — it is the permanent record of how
the contact got into Dhaga in the first place, so it doesn't count toward your
notes either.

Want to add outside context to a person? See
[Enrich from the web](/docs/guide/enrich-from-web).
