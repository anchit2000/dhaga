## Network Wrapped

**`/app/wrapped`** (nav **Wrapped**) is a shareable, year-in-review-style recap of
your networking — "47 people met this month", "strongest cluster: fintech". Every
figure is computed deterministically from your own graph with plain SQL, so
there's no AI cost and nothing to meter. It's part of the core, and works the same
on a self-hosted instance.

- **Pick a scope.** The scope picker recaps a single **event**, or a rolling
  window — **this week**, **this month**, **this quarter**, **this year** (the
  default), or **all time**.
- **Pick a format.** Export the card as a **Post** (landscape), **Square**, or
  **Story** — the aspect ratios for a feed, a grid, or a full-screen story.
- **Share it.** Download the image, copy the public link, or hand off to your
  device's share sheet.

<Callout title="Names never leave your device unless you choose">
The card is contact-free by construction: it shows aggregate counts and category
superlatives only. The two name-bearing figures — your top company and
most-connected person — stay hidden behind a **Show my names** toggle that reveals
them **in-app, for you only**. They never enter the share token, the link, or the
image, so a card you post leaks nothing about the people in your graph.
</Callout>

## Invite friends

**`/app/referral`** (nav **Invite friends**) is a two-sided referral: share your
link and both you and the friend who joins get a **free month of Pro**. A valid
invite also admits them past the early-access wall.

<Callout title="A hosted Dhaga Cloud feature">
Referrals live in the hosted Dhaga Cloud tier — they extend a subscription, which
the core has no concept of, and a self-host is single-user anyway (there's nobody
to refer). On a self-hosted instance the referral surface is simply absent. See
[Self-hosting](/docs/self-hosting).
</Callout>
