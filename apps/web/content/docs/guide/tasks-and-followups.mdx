Open **Tasks** at **`/app/tasks`** for work that may not belong to a person. A
task needs only a title; its due date, person, and company are optional. Use
**Active** and **Completed** to work the list, edit details, or mark it done. A
dated task also appears on [Calendar](/docs/guide/calendar); an undated one stays
in Tasks instead of receiving a made-up date.

![The Tasks list showing general, person-linked, company-linked, dated, and unscheduled work](/docs/guide/scheduling/tasks-light-desktop.webp)

## Repeat work without duplicating it

Tasks and person follow-ups can repeat daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly. Choose
the interval and, where relevant, the weekday, day of month, or month and day.
Completing the current occurrence advances the same item exactly once;
dismissing it ends the series. Short months clamp to their final day.

![A weekly task editor with an optional company and a chosen weekday](/docs/guide/scheduling/recurring-task-light-desktop.webp)

## Dates mentioned in notes

When a note says *“reach out by Saturday”*, *“next Monday”*, or *“10 days from
now”*, Dhaga resolves it with calendar code rather than asking an LLM to do date
arithmetic. *“Next weekend”* schedules Saturday immediately, then adds a
[Confirmation](/docs/guide/confirmations) where you can keep Saturday or move it
to Sunday.

## Keep-in-touch schedules

- Weekly and fortnightly: optional weekday.
- Monthly and quarterly: optional day of month.
- Twice yearly and yearly: optional month and day.

Choose **Auto** for a weekly cadence and Dhaga spreads people across the week
using **People/day**, then saves the assigned weekday. An explicit crowded day
shows a warning before anything is saved; choose **Save anyway** to keep that
weekday, or cancel to restore the previous schedule. Due dates follow your
Settings time zone; older elapsed-day-only contacts continue to work unchanged.

![A mobile Keep in touch card warning that Monday is over capacity before Save anyway](/docs/guide/scheduling/keep-in-touch-warning-dark-mobile.webp)

<Callout title="One calendar, two kinds of work">
Person follow-ups and general tasks share calendar and completion rules. Linking
context is optional; Dhaga never invents a placeholder person.
</Callout>

## When a link goes nowhere

If a saved link points to a page that moved or has not been built yet, Dhaga
shows the cloth-shop search scene instead of a generic error. From there you can
open the app or return to the homepage. The same recovery actions work inside
the signed-in app and on public pages, in light and dark themes.
